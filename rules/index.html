The Rules

How to get set up, how each part of the site works, and what counts as a win.

1 · GET SET UP

How to Set Up

  1. Get your login.Your Goblin Investing account is tied to your email.
  2. Buy at least $5 of stock every week.The homepage shows the current week and your buy-in to date. Week 6 means you must have bought at least $30 of stock total. Current portfolio value does not matter.
  3. Play Win the Week.Each week, the best percentage return wins $1.
  4. Add "Called It!" challenges when you have a real reason.These are separate from the weekly competition and reward doing research before making a prediction.
2 · HOMEPAGE

What Each Section Is

"Called It!"

Everyone's current due diligence challenges. Sign in to create, check, or manage your own.

Current Lesson

Investing articles are featured here. They are reference material, not part of the competition.

The Receipts

Past weekly winners and "Called It!" submissions. A Called It can be Under Review, Approved, or Rejected. Only Approved challenges count toward Most Called Its.

3 · "CALLED IT!"

"Called It!"

Due Diligence Challenges
Due diligence means researching a stock before making a decision. A Called It challenge records what you think will happen, why you think it, and what you are doing with the investment before the result is known.

Make a challenge

  1. Choose a stock. The site records its current price for you.
  2. Choose Go Up, Go Down, or Finish About the Same.
  3. Explain why you think it will happen. Links are allowed.
  4. Record what you are doing: Buying, Holding, Selling, or Not Buying. Buy, Hold, and Sell require at least $5.

The goals

  • Go Up: the stock reaches 15% above the starting price within 28 days.
  • Go Down: the stock reaches 15% below the starting price within 28 days.
  • Finish About the Same: after 28 days, the stock is within 3% above or below the starting price.

Check the result

  • Use Check Price on an active challenge to see where it stands.
  • Go Up and Go Down can reach their goal at any price check during the 28-day challenge.
  • Finish About the Same cannot qualify early. It is checked after the challenge ends, with 7 days to claim it.
  • If the goal is met, Submit for Review appears.
  • The price is checked again when you submit. If the stock moved back outside the goal, it is not submitted yet.

Review and payout

  • Submitting changes the challenge to Under Review.
  • That challenge slot is locked for 7 days after submission.
  • An Approved Called It pays $5 and counts toward Most Called Its.
  • A Rejected, Cancelled, or Expired challenge pays $0.
  • You can have up to two challenge slots. You cannot make another active call on a ticker you already have active.
Cancel: You can abandon an active challenge. Cancelled challenges do not pay. Once a challenge is submitted for review, its prediction is locked.
4 · WEEKLY COMPETITION

Win the Week

  • Win the Week is separate from "Called It!".
  • You must stay current on the weekly buy-in requirement to keep playing.
  • The person with the best portfolio percentage return for the week wins $1.
