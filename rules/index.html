The Rules

How to get set up, how each part of the site works, and what counts as a win.

1 · GET SET UP

How to Set Up

  1. Get your login.Your Goblin Investing account is tied to your email.
  2. Buy at least $5 of stock every week.The homepage shows the current week and your buy-in to date. Week 6 means you must have bought at least $30 of stock total. Current portfolio value does not matter.
  3. Use the same portfolio for Win the Week.Each week, the best percentage return wins $1.
  4. Add Called It! challenges when you have a real reason.These are separate from the weekly competition and reward good research.
2 · HOMEPAGE

What Each Section Is

Win the Week

The current weekly winner, their portfolio return, and the chart used for that week's result.

Called It! (Due Diligence Challenges)

Everyone's active research challenges. You can see all challenges, but only edit your own.

Current Lesson

The current investing lesson. This is separate from the competitions.

The Receipts

Past weekly winners and successful Called It! payouts, plus the all-time leaders.

3 · DUE DILIGENCE

Called It! (Due Diligence Challenges)

Make the challenge before the move happens and write down the research behind it.

Making a challenge

  • You can have up to two active challenges at once.
  • Commit at least $5 of your portfolio to the stock.
  • Record the challenge before the move happens.
  • Write what you found and what you think will happen.

Winning a challenge

  • The target is +10% from the recorded call price.
  • The challenge expires after four weeks.
  • You must keep owning the committed amount while the challenge is active.
  • The move should reasonably match the reason you wrote down. Crossing +10% by itself does not automatically count.
  • A confirmed Called It! pays $5 and goes into The Receipts.
Cancel or replace: You can abandon a challenge at any time. Cancelled or expired challenges do not pay.
4 · WEEKLY COMPETITION

Win the Week

  • Win the Week is separate from Called It!.
  • You must stay current on the weekly buy-in requirement to keep playing.
  • The person with the best portfolio percentage return for the week wins $1.
  • The winner and chart are entered by the admin. The site does not connect to brokerage accounts.
  • Every weekly win is saved permanently in The Receipts.
ADMIN

Who Decides?

The admin posts each weekly result and decides whether a Called It! challenge qualified based on the recorded thesis. When a Called It! is cashed out, the challenge closes and the win is added to The Receipts.
