THE RULES

Keep it simple.

WIN THE WEEK

Best weekly portfolio return wins.

  • The weekly competition is separate from Called It!.
  • The person with the best portfolio return for the week wins $1.
  • The weekly result and chart are entered manually. The site does not connect to brokerage accounts.
CALLED IT!

Make the call before the move.

  • You can have no more than two active calls at a time.
  • You must commit at least $5 to each call and keep owning that amount while the call is active.
  • Write the call down before the move happens.
  • The target is +10% from the recorded call price.
  • Each call expires four weeks after the call date.
  • A successful move should reasonably match the reason you wrote down. Price movement alone does not automatically qualify.
  • You can cancel or replace a call at any time. Cancelled or expired calls do not pay.
  • A confirmed successful Called It! pays $5.
WHO DECIDES?

The admin confirms Called It! wins.

The site records the call, target, expiration, and thesis. It does not automatically award the bonus just because a stock crossed the target.
