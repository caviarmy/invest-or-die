GOBLIN INVESTING

How it works.

Buy stock every week, compete on performance, and get paid extra when your research turns into a good call.

1 · GET SET UP

Start here.

  1. Get your login.Your Goblin Investing account is tied to your email.
  2. Buy at least $5 of stock every week.The homepage shows the current week and your total purchase minimum. Week 6 means you must have bought at least $30 of stock total. What those shares are worth today does not change the requirement.
  3. Use the same portfolio for Win the Week.Each week, the best percentage return wins $1.
  4. Add Called It! plays when you have a real reason.These are separate from the weekly competition and reward good research.
2 · WHAT'S ON THE HOMEPAGE

Know what you're looking at.

Win the Week

The current weekly winner, their portfolio return, and the chart used for that week's result.

Called It! Plays

Everyone's active due-diligence calls. You can see all plays, but only edit your own.

Current Lesson

The current investing lesson. This is separate from the competition.

History

Past weekly winners and successful Called It! payouts, plus the all-time leaders.

3 · CALLED IT! (DUE DILIGENCE)

Research it, write it down, then see if you were right.

Making a call

  • You can have up to two active calls at once.
  • Commit at least $5 of your portfolio to the stock.
  • Record the call before the move happens.
  • Write what you found and what you think will happen.

Winning a call

  • The target is +10% from the recorded call price.
  • The call expires after four weeks.
  • You must keep owning the committed amount while the call is active.
  • The move should reasonably match the reason you wrote down. Crossing +10% by itself does not automatically count.
  • A confirmed Called It! pays $5 and goes into the history table.
Cancel or replace: You can abandon a call at any time. Cancelled or expired calls do not pay.
4 · WIN THE WEEK

Best percentage return wins.

  • Win the Week is a separate competition from Called It!.
  • You must stay current on the weekly stock purchase minimum to keep playing.
  • The person with the best portfolio percentage return for the week wins $1.
  • The winner and chart are entered by the admin. The site does not connect to brokerage accounts.
  • Every weekly win is saved permanently in History.
ADMIN

What gets confirmed manually.

The admin posts each weekly result and decides whether a Called It! actually qualified based on the recorded thesis. When a Called It! is cashed out, the play closes and the win is added to History.
