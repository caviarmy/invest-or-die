The Rules

01

Start Here

  1. Get your login.Your account is tied to your email.
  2. Buy stock every week.The homepage shows the current weekly buy-in and the required total to date. Current portfolio value does not matter.
  3. Play Win the Week.Best percentage return for the week wins $1.
  4. File Called It challenges when you have a reason.They are separate from the weekly contest and record the prediction before the result is known.
02

Called It

Due diligence: research a stock before making a decision. A challenge records what you think will happen, why, and what you are doing with the investment before the result is known.

Loading current Called It settings…

File the call

  • Choose a stock. The site records its current price.
  • Choose Go Up, Go Down, or Finish About the Same.
  • Explain why. Links are allowed.
  • Record what you are doing: Buying, Holding, Selling, or Not Buying. Buy, Hold, and Sell require at least $5.

Qualification

  • Go Up: Current target unavailable.
  • Go Down: Current target unavailable.
  • About the Same: Current range unavailable.

Check and submit

  • Use Check Price on an active challenge.
  • Up and Down can qualify at any price check during the current challenge.
  • About the Same cannot qualify early. It is checked after the challenge ends, with the current claim window to claim it.
  • If the goal is met, Submit for Review appears.
  • Submission checks the price again. If it moved outside the goal, the challenge is not submitted yet.

Review and payout

  • Submission changes the challenge to Under Review.
  • The slot is locked for the current review cooldown after submission.
  • An Approved Called It pays the current payout and counts toward the leaderboard.
  • Rejected, Cancelled, and Expired challenges pay $0.
  • You have up to two challenge slots. You cannot make another active call on a ticker you already have active.

Cancel: An active challenge can be abandoned and pays nothing. Once submitted for review, its prediction is locked.

03

Win the Week

  • Win the Week is separate from Called It.
  • Staying current on the weekly buy-in is a household rule. The site shows the requirement; it does not verify brokerage purchases or block play.
  • Best portfolio percentage return for the week wins $1.
PORTFOLIO VALUE DOES NOT DECIDE THE WINNER.
